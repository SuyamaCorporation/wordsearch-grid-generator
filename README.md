# Word Search Puzzle Grid Generator

Enter up to 15 words and instantly generate a printable word search puzzle with a randomly filled letter grid. Words are hidden in 8 directions (horizontal, vertical, 4 diagonals, and all reversed). Click and drag across letters to highlight and check off found words while a live timer tracks your solving time.

## Features

- Up to 15 custom words placed in 8 directions including diagonals and reversed
- Click-drag selection to highlight and check off found words
- Live timer that stops when all words are found
- Configurable grid size (15×15, 20×20, 25×25)
- Print-friendly layout that hides controls for clean output
